Sort Duel — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Sort Duel does not ask for an account, an email address, or a real name, and it has no advertising and no analytics. This page describes everything the app stores or sends.

What stays on your device

  • Which level you have reached, your marks and your best score on each level.
  • Your sound, haptics and button-side preferences.
  • The last three error messages, if the app has failed, so a problem can be described.
  • A random player identifier, kept in the device keychain so it survives reinstalling. It is a random number and is not derived from you or your device.

None of this leaves the device unless you play a mode that needs another person.

What is sent, and when

Playing online, or putting a score on a leaderboard, sends the following to our server: the random player identifier, the nickname you chose, your score, and a record of the round — the belt's seed and the times of your button presses. The round record is what lets the server check a score is real and lets other players race a saved run.

A nickname is optional and is shown to other players. Please do not put anything private in it.

Where it is kept

On Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany. Leaderboard entries and saved runs are deleted automatically after a short period. No data is sold, and none is shared with anyone except AWS, which hosts it on our behalf.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Removing your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device, including the identifier — after which nothing on the server can be connected to you. To have leaderboard entries removed sooner, write to the address below and include the identifier shown on the app's Settings screen.
