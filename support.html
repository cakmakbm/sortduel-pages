Write to cakmakberatmetehan@gmail.com. It helps a great deal to include the build number and player id from the app's Settings screen, and — if the app failed — a photograph of whatever it showed.

How to play

One object at a time arrives at the scanner. The TAKE button wears a picture of what the belt wants: press TAKE for that, PASS for everything else. From level 11 the belt changes its mind partway through, and it says so.

Common questions

  • A level ended early. Every level gives you a number of hearts and a mistake costs one. The results screen says whether it was the hearts or the clock.
  • Quick match cannot find anyone. After a short wait you are given a saved run by another player to race instead. It is a real round somebody played.
  • My progress is gone. Progress is kept on the device. Deleting the app, or a system short of storage, can clear it; it is not on our server and cannot be restored.
  • Reporting a nickname. Names are filtered when they are set, but if you see one on a leaderboard that should not be there, tap Report a name under the standings and then tap the name. Nothing is typed and nothing leaves the app. Reported entries are removed from the board, and the name is refused after that.
